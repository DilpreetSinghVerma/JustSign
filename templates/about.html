About Us


The Automatic Translator for Complex English Sentences To Indian Sign Language Synthetic Video Animations tool is developed by Deepali (P.hD. Research Scholar), Department of Computer Science, Punjabi University, Patiala under the Leadership of Dr. Vishal Goyal (Professor), Department of Computer Science, Punjabi University, Patiala and Dr. Lalit Goyal (Associate Professor), D.A.V. College, Jalandhar.

Special help of Ms. Karishma Dhariwal, Research Assistant (Interpreter cum Signer) for Indian Sign Language.

This tool can be used to bridge the communication gap between hearing and speech impaired persons and normal persons.
